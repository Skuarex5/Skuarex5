## About Me

I'm **Santiago Fernández**, a developer interested in building practical solutions at the intersection of **software, robotics, automation, and interactive technology**.

I enjoy turning ideas into working projects, experimenting with new tools, and learning through hands-on development.

As a member of **UruBots 🇺🇾**, I'm especially interested in how software and engineering come together in real-world robotic systems.

- **Currently exploring:** Python, C#, ROS, artificial intelligence, and robotics.
- **Interested in:** Autonomous systems, software tools, embedded applications, and game development.
- **I enjoy:** Collaborative projects, experimentation, and technical problem-solving.
- **Open to:** Collaborating on interesting open-source and engineering projects.
